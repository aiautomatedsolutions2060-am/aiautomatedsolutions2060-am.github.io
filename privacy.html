Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

This policy explains how the No Ordinary Words publishing tool (“the tool”, “we”, “us”) handles information when it connects to YouTube and TikTok. The tool is a private application that the owner of the No Ordinary Words channel uses to post the channel's own videos to its own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When the channel owner connects an account, the tool asks only for the permissions it needs to post videos:

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and set their thumbnails (youtube.upload), and to read the channel's name and the status of its uploads (youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok: the account's basic profile information, such as its display name and open ID (user.info.basic), permission to post videos to that account, and the posting options TikTok allows for it, such as the privacy levels it can use (video.publish).

The tool does not read viewers' information, comments, messages, contacts, subscriber lists or any other account data.

How we use it

  • To upload the channel's own videos with their titles, descriptions, hashtags, privacy settings and cover images.
  • To show the channel owner which account is connected and whether each upload succeeded.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, analytics or training machine-learning models, and we do not sell, rent or trade it.

Where it is stored

The sign-in tokens that YouTube and TikTok issue, the connected account's name, and a log of uploads (video IDs, links, privacy setting and status) are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server of ours, because there is none. The tool talks directly to Google's and TikTok's official APIs.

Sharing

We do not share data obtained from YouTube or TikTok with anyone. Videos and their text are sent only to the platform they are being posted to.

Google API Services

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads to YouTube are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

Posts to TikTok are subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Sign-in tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. Disconnecting deletes them from the tool. Access can also be removed at any time from the platform itself:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services permissions

The upload log is kept for the channel's own records and can be deleted on request.

Security

Tokens are kept in a local file on the channel owner's computer that is not shared or synced to other services, and all connections to Google and TikTok use HTTPS.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
