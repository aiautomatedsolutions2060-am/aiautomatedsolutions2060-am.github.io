Real words from people who paid for them.

No Ordinary Words makes short spoken-word films from the recorded and published words of people such as Malcolm X, Muhammad Ali, Miyamoto Musashi, Thomas Sankara, Napoleon Hill and Brian Tracy. Each film takes what they said and turns it into something you can use today.

The narration and music are made with AI. The channel has no connection to the people featured or their estates.

The No Ordinary Words publishing tool

No Ordinary Words uses a private publishing tool to post its own videos to its own YouTube and TikTok accounts. The tool runs on the channel owner's computer and is not offered to the public. It signs in with YouTube and TikTok only to upload our videos with their titles, descriptions and cover images, and to read back the result of each upload.

How the tool handles account data is set out in the Privacy Policy, and the terms for using it in the Terms of Service.
